\section{Related Work}

Classical models of prime gaps include probabilistic heuristics such as Cramér's model~\cite{cramer1936} and analytic frameworks for prime distributions~\cite{hardy1979}.

Markov models provide a baseline for transition structure~\cite{aldous1983}, while information-theoretic measures quantify higher-order structure relative to first-order statistics~\cite{cover2006}.


This work analyzes residue-class transitions of consecutive primes using empirical transition operators and the residual operator $\Delta = P^{(2)} - P^2$.
