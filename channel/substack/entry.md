# A Small Matrix View of Prime Transitions

Consecutive primes can be reduced modulo 30, producing a sequence over 8 states.

From this, we construct a transition operator P and compare it with observed two-step behavior.

The difference:

Δ = P^(2) − P²

captures structure not explained by a first-order model.

Across large samples, Δ is low-rank.

This concentrates structure into a small number of components.

This note presents a computational view, not a proof, and suggests a direction for connecting local transition structure with broader models of primes.
