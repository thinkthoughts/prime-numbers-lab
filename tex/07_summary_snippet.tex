\section*{Constraint Reconstruction}

This notebook reconstructs candidate prime structure from corrupted observations and stress tests global density reconstruction.

Precision and recovery are defined by
\[
precision = \frac{|\hat{P}_N\cap P_N|}{|\hat{P}_N|},
\qquad
recovery = \frac{|\hat{P}_N\cap P_N|}{|P_N|}.
\]

The $F_1$ score is
\[
F_1 = \frac{2\cdot precision\cdot recovery}{precision+recovery}.
\]

Gap drift is approximated by
\[
drift_{\mathrm{gap}}=\operatorname{mean}_i \frac{|g_i-\log p_i|}{\log p_i}.
\]

For $N=200,000$:
\begin{itemize}
  \item mean raw precision $= 0.441667$
  \item mean sieve-filter precision $= 1.000000$
  \item mean reconstruction recovery $= 0.990881$
  \item mean reconstruction precision $= 1.000000$
  \item mean reconstruction $F_1 = 0.995420$
  \item mean reconstruction gap drift $= 0.587789$
\end{itemize}

Filtering restores precision; reconstruction trades precision for recovery.
