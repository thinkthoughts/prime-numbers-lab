\section*{Prime Density vs x/log(x)}

This notebook compares prime counts to the logarithmic density baseline
\[
\pi(x) \approx \frac{x}{\log x}.
\]

For primes up to 1,000,000:
\begin{itemize}
  \item final ratio $\pi(x)/(x/\log x) = 1.084490$
  \item mean density drift $= 0.133313$
  \item median density drift $= 0.128925$
  \item $CGCS_{density} = 0.882369$
\end{itemize}

The baseline $x/\log x$ recovers global density scale, not exact prime locations.
