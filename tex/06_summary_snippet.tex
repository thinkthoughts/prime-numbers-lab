\section*{Recoverability Under Partial Observation}

This notebook corrupts prime observations and measures which constraint signals remain recoverable.

The recovery score is
\[
CGCS_{\mathrm{recovery}}(S)=
\frac{|S\cap P_N|}{|P_N|}.
\]

The precision score is
\[
precision(S)=
\frac{|S\cap P_N|}{|S|}.
\]

For $N=200,000$:
\begin{itemize}
  \item baseline recovery $= 1.000000$
  \item baseline precision $= 1.000000$
  \item worst mixed recovery $= 0.250000$
  \item worst mixed precision $= 0.200000$
\end{itemize}

Constraint structure remains detectable after corruption.
