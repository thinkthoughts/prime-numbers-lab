\section*{Normalized Gap Distribution}

Notebook 12 measures normalized prime gaps against an exponential baseline.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Prime count below $N$: 78498
  \item Gap count after cutoff: 78472
  \item Overall mean normalized gap: 1.001590
  \item Overall standard deviation of normalized gap: 0.798099
  \item Final-window CGCS score: 0.977480
\end{itemize}

The normalized gap variable is

\[
z_n = \frac{p_{n+1} - p_n}{\log(p_n)}.
\]

The baseline comparison is

\[
Z \sim \mathrm{Exp}(1).
\]
