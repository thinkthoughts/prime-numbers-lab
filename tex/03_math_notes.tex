\section*{Math Notes: Prime Density vs x/log(x)}

\subsection*{Prime-counting function}

Let $\pi(x)$ denote the number of primes less than or equal to $x$.

\subsection*{Logarithmic density baseline}

\[
\pi(x) \approx \frac{x}{\log x}.
\]

\subsection*{Ratio}

\[
R(x) = \frac{\pi(x)}{x/\log x}.
\]

\subsection*{Density drift}

\[
drift(x) =
\frac{|\pi(x) - x/\log x|}{x/\log x}.
\]

\subsection*{CGCS score}

\[
CGCS_{density} =
\frac{1}{1 + \operatorname{mean}(drift(x))}.
\]

Measured value:
\[
CGCS_{density} = 0.882369.
\]

\subsection*{Recoverability}

The baseline $x/\log x$ recovers global count scale, not exact prime locations.
