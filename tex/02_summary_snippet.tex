\section*{Prime Gaps}

This notebook studies prime gaps
\[
g_n = p_{n+1} - p_n
\]
and compares them to the logarithmic scale baseline
\[
\mathbb{E}[g_n] \approx \log(p_n).
\]

For primes below 1,000,000, using gaps with $p_n \ge 100$:
\begin{itemize}
  \item mean gap $= 12.741895$
  \item mean $\log(p_n) = 12.722839$
  \item mean relative drift $= 0.599699$
  \item $CGCS_{gap} = 0.625118$
\end{itemize}

Logarithmic scaling recovers coarse gap scale, not exact gaps.
